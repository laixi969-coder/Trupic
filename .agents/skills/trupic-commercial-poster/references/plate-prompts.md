Place the entire glass on the right at x=68% and y=65%, glass occupies 36% of composition width and 40% height, photographed at low three-quarter level. Continuous velvety olive-black to rich moss-green studio cyclorama, side lighting like an architectural slit of afternoon sunlight in an old tea room, translucent green liquid glows like jade, subtly tactile analog film. Left 36% and upper 28% are very calm dark forest-green negative space with a softly blurred diagonal of light, ready for extra-large white Chinese typography which will be laid in later. Distinct sculptural shadow falls bottom left, sugar-white milky ribbons swirl inside, bright soft rim highlight, high-end beverage advertising art direction, punchy but real, camera sharp glass no fake packaging. Full-bleed photographed image as a single continuous environment, no panels, no collage, no mockup.
